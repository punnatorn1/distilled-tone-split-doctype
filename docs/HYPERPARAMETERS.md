[Back to the README](../README.md) · Explained in [5. The models](5_models.md), section 5.6.

Generated by `python scripts/make_results_tables.py` from `results/*/networks/settings_*.json`; do not edit by hand. One row per tuning point (the first meeting of the year; the settings are used until the next point). "layers/heads" is the LSTM or GRU layer count, or the TFT attention head count. "CV log loss" is the mean validation log loss of the 4 expanding folds for the chosen setting. "trials" counts the 20 Optuna trials that could be scored (a trial whose lookback leaves too few training sequences is skipped). Settings jump between points (an LSTM's hidden size can go from 64 to 256) because there are so few meetings that tuning is unstable.
