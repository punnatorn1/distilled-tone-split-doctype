The tables in this chapter are written by `python scripts/make_results_tables.py` straight from the files in
`results/`; nothing in them is typed by hand. The prose around them interprets them. "S+M+Hd" is the single distilled
tone of attempt 12/12b, **the main result of this repository** (since 1 November 2026); "S+M+Hs" is the split tone of
attempt 14/14b, now the secondary result.

## 7.1 The verdict in one paragraph

**Single tone Hd (main result), all four models from 2010:** adding the two tone columns raised accuracy and lowered
log loss in every model (ordered logit: log loss 0.356 → 0.320, accuracy 83.7% → 85.9%). No model passes both checks
(real tone beats no news, and real tone beats its placebo while the placebo does not beat no news). Ordered logit:
one-sided Clark-West p = 0.220 against no news (its pre-registered test: not supported). TFT: beats no news (Newey-West,
Holm over 3 networks, p = 0.0050) but not its placebo (p = 0.082). LSTM: beats its placebo (p = 0.032) but not no news
(Holm p = 0.220). GRU: neither.
**Split tone Hs (secondary):** the ordered logit with the six split columns beat no news from 2010 (one-sided
Clark-West **p = 0.0100**; log loss 0.356 → 0.322) and its placebo did not (p = 0.456), so its own pre-registered
decision is "supported", worded as *exploratory* because this is the 14th attempt. The Hs networks are not
significant after Holm (LSTM 0.297, GRU 0.372, TFT 0.408). **Split vs single tone:** the six columns did not forecast
measurably better than the two-column single tone for any model.

## 7.3 Reading the ordered logit

- **Nothing is void.** The placebo is far from significant (p = 0.456 from 2010) and the shuffled-label control even
  further (p = 0.933). The test machinery behaves as it should.
- **Hs passes; Hd, with almost the same log loss, does not.** S+M+Hd reaches log loss 0.320 and S+M+Hs 0.322, yet
  Clark-West gives 0.220 for Hd and 0.0100 for Hs. Why (examined after the results were known, so this is
  description, not evidence):
  - the mean gain per meeting is about the same: 0.034 (Hs) and 0.036 (Hd) in log loss;
  - the Hs gain is **steadier**: the standard deviation of the per-meeting difference is 0.21 against 0.35 for Hd;
  - $t$ = mean ÷ standard error, so the same mean with less spread gives a larger $t$ and a smaller $p$;
  - Hd lost most at the two emergency meetings of March 2020 (3 and 15 March). Without those two meetings, Clark-West
    is 0.0098 (Hs) and 0.058 (Hd).
- **Split vs single.** Hs against Hd directly: Clark-West p = 0.071, DM-HLN on log loss p = 0.544. **The split did not
  make the forecasts measurably better; it made the gain steadier.**
- **Full period (212 meetings, no decision rule):** Clark-West p = 0.0102, placebo 0.914, shuffled 0.993. Note that
  before 2010 the distilled reader scores sentences with cross-fitted models (chapter 3.7), so the full period mixes
  two kinds of tone.
- **The gain sits in a few meetings** (chapter 6.7): Hs beats S+M at 72 of 135 meetings; the largest single gain is 29
  July 2020, where S+M was confidently wrong.

## 7.4 Reading the networks

- **The networks are much worse than the ordered logit.** From 2010, the S+M log loss is 0.403 (TFT), 0.560 (GRU),
  0.665 (LSTM), against 0.356 for the ordered logit. The main reason is too many parameters for too few meetings
  (chapter 4.3).
- **A weak baseline makes any addition look good.** Across every placebo comparison against S+M in the project (4
  placebo types × 3 networks × 2 periods = 24 pairs), the placebo had a lower mean log loss than S+M in 22.
- **Training randomness is larger than the effect measured.** From 2010, the TFT S+M log loss of single seeds ranges
  from 0.384 to 0.581, while the mean gain of the TFT with placebo Hs over S+M is 0.058. Newey-West counts only the
  variation across meetings, not the variation from seeds and tuning.
- **The TFT placebo Hs beat S+M (p = 0.047).** This comes from a single meeting, 2 May 2018, where S+M gave the hold
  that happened only 0.028 and the placebo arm gave 0.230. Without that meeting, p = 0.160.
- **Accuracy and log loss disagree.** The TFT with Hs has the highest accuracy (92.5%) but a worse log loss than the
  TFT with Hd (0.391 against 0.318), because when it is wrong it is more confidently wrong.
- **Only the GRU passes the placebo reading** (real Hs beats placebo Hs at p = 0.010, and the placebo does not beat
  S+M), but its gain over S+M is not significant (p = 0.186, Holm 0.372).
- **Hd (attempt 12b) for comparison:** the TFT with Hd has the lowest network log loss (0.318) and beats S+M after Holm
  (0.0050), but not its own placebo (p = 0.082), so the gain cannot be attributed to the tone's content.
